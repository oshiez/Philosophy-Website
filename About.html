About The Philosophy Website

This is an educational website where people can learn about philosophical topics without being bombarded with confusing jargon. It includes everyday examples that are easily relatable, and gets to the point that most students skip to. It primarily involves argumentation.

What's its goal?

To provide people around the world solid information about philosophy, one article at a time.

How was this website made?

This website was made with HTML, CSS, and JS.

When will it be getting updates?

Updates will be released every 2 months or less.
