About the Author

Who am I?

Hello! I am Deklin Trail, the author of this website. I am a young developer eager to teach people like you philosophy. I have 6 years of programming experience across a wide variety of languages; including but not limited to: HTML, CSS, JS, C#, C++, Python, Lua, and Luau. I also have been rigorously learning Philosophy, and implementing a simplified summary into my site everytime I learn something new!

Why did I make this website?

I want people to be able to have a simplified understanding of philosophy, not with all of those fancy words that modern textbooks or dictionaries use.
