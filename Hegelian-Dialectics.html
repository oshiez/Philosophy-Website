Hegelian Dialectics

The Hegelian Dialectics focuses on how people interact with one another and come to form a conclusion. It shows that arguments and conversations do not flow in one nice manner, and disagreeing is part of the process.

Thesis

A thesis is an idea or goal, and it encompasses all of somebody's points on a given topic.

Antithesis

An antithesis is an opposing opinion or disagreement with the thesis, and is the other side of the argument.

Synthesis

A synthesis is a new resolve or combination of elements from the thesis and the antithesis to form a higher truth.
